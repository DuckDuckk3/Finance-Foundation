# Finance-Foundation

**Finance-Foundation** is a personal knowledge repository for finance and quantitative research.  
Its goal is to **collect, organize, and share** what I have learned in financial analysis, modeling, and strategy development.

## Purpose

- Aggregate financial concepts, formulas, and datasets  
- Share insights and example strategies with others  
- Provide simple tools for analysis and experimentation  
- Serve as a reference for learning and research

## Learning Path 

1. **Corporate Strategy**  
   - Business models, network effects, switching costs  
   - Capital allocation and management strategy  
   - Economic moat and competitive advantage  

2. **Accounting & Finance**  
   - Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement  
   - Revenue models, Gross/Operating/Net Margin, Free Cash Flow  
   - Debt, leverage, ROE, ROIC, ROIC vs Cost of Capital  

3. **Valuation**  
   - P/E, EV/EBITDA, PEG ratio  
   - Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) basics  

4. **Risk Management**  
   - Fundamentals, Value at Risk (VaR), stress testing  
   - Hedging strategies  

5. **Mathematics & Statistics**  
   - Probability, descriptive statistics, correlation and causation  
   - Hypothesis testing, regression basics  

6. **Economics**  
   - Microeconomics: supply & demand, market structures, elasticity  
   - Macroeconomics: GDP, inflation, interest rates, monetary & fiscal policy  

7. **Econometrics**  
   - Regression and time series fundamentals  

8. **Machine Learning (ML)**  
   - ML concepts for finance will be covered **in a separate repository**
